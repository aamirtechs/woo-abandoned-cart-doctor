Woo Abandoned Cart Doctor

A zero-dependency Python CLI that audits a WooCommerce abandoned carts CSV export for recoverable value: high-value carts with a reachable email, repeat abandoners, carts abandoned after checkout started, carts with no email at all (unrecoverable), stale carts past the recovery window, duplicate carts for one customer, and bad data such as duplicate cart ids, malformed rows, invalid amounts, and invalid or future dates. It totals the recoverable and unrecoverable value in the export, so a store owner can see which carts are worth chasing before sending a single recovery email.
